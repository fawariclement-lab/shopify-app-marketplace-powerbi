# Shopify App Marketplace Performance Analysis

**Tools:** Power BI · DAX · Data Modeling · Dashboard Development

## Overview

The Shopify app marketplace lists thousands of apps, and merchants choosing between them rely on ratings and reviews that are hard to compare at a glance. This project analyzes **7,300+ Shopify apps** in Power BI to evaluate marketplace trends, review activity, and ratings — and to identify the factors associated with strong app performance, including how helpful an app's reviews are and how responsive its developer is.

## What I built

- Power BI dashboards covering marketplace trends, review activity, and ratings across 7,300+ apps
- **DAX metrics** assessing review helpfulness and developer responsiveness, so quality signals go beyond a raw star rating
- Comparative views that surface patterns in ratings and engagement to support app evaluation and marketplace decisions

## Key findings

- Ratings and engagement vary widely across the marketplace — headline star ratings alone are a weak guide; review helpfulness and developer responsiveness separate consistently strong apps from the rest
- Review activity concentrates on a subset of apps, so most listings compete with relatively little social proof — a visibility problem as much as a quality one
- The responsiveness metrics give merchants a practical shortlisting signal: apps whose developers engage with reviews tend to present a measurably different ratings profile
